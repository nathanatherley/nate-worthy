What Nate-Worthy is

Nate-Worthy is a restaurant recommendation app built on trust between real people. You rate restaurants, rate how much you trust the taste of people in your circle, and get recommendations that weigh those trusted opinions more heavily than a generic star average.

Your account

  • You're responsible for keeping access to your own email, since anyone with access to it can sign in as you.
  • Accounts are for individual people, not businesses, bots, or bulk-created identities.
  • You must be at least 13 years old to use Nate-Worthy.

What you post

  • You're responsible for what you write and upload — restaurant names, notes, ratings, and photos. Post honestly: real opinions about places you've actually been, not fabricated reviews.
  • Don't post anything illegal, harassing, hateful, sexually explicit, or that infringes someone else's rights (including copyrighted photos you didn't take).
  • Don't impersonate another person or restaurant.
  • We may review and remove content that violates these rules, including through automated review, and may suspend accounts that repeatedly violate them.
  • You keep ownership of what you post, but by posting it you're giving Nate-Worthy permission to display it to other users within the app — that's the whole point of sharing a recommendation.

Trust ratings

The star rating you give another person reflects your own private opinion of their taste in restaurants, for your own use in weighting recommendations. It isn't a public statement about that person, and only you can see who you've rated and how.

Restaurants close, change hands, and change menus faster than any database can keep up with. Always use your own judgment, and double-check details like hours or dietary accommodations directly with the restaurant when it matters.
