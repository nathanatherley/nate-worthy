PRIVACY POLICY — LAST UPDATED AUGUST 26, 2026

This is a plain-language description of how Nate-Worthy handles your information, written by the person who built the app rather than a law firm. It's meant to be genuinely readable, not a document designed to be skimmed past. If anything here is unclear, reach out through the feedback link in the app.

What we collect

Nate-Worthy is a restaurant recommendation app built around trust between real people, so it collects what it needs to make that work:

  • Your name and email address, when you sign up.
  • Your phone number, only if you choose to invite a friend by text or provide it yourself — it's never required to use the app.
  • Your location, only when you allow your browser to share it, and only to suggest your home city or show how far a restaurant is from you. You can decline location access entirely and still use the app — you'll just type your city in manually.
  • The restaurants you rate, including any notes, star ratings, and photos you choose to attach.
  • Who you trust, and how much — the 1-to-5 star trust ratings you give people in your circle. These are private: only you ever see the trust rating you've given someone.
  • Basic usage information, like when you sign up, post an entry, or run a recommendation search — used only to understand whether the app is working well, not to track your individual browsing in detail.

What we don't collect

  • We don't buy or combine your data with information from data brokers or other companies.
  • We don't track you across other websites or apps.
  • We don't require a password — sign-in works through a one-time emailed link instead, so we never store a password for you to worry about being leaked.

How your information is used

  • To generate your recommendations. Your trust ratings, your circle's ratings, and (when you ask) your search text are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to help find and rank restaurant suggestions for you.
  • To look up restaurant details. When you add or view a restaurant, we look up its address, phone number, and location through Google's Places API. Google's own privacy terms govern that lookup.
  • To send you a sign-in link or an invite you've asked us to send to a friend, via our email provider, Resend.
  • To store your photos, if you attach any to an entry, using Cloudflare's storage service.
  • To catch and fix bugs — if the app encounters an error, basic technical details about that error are sent to Sentry, our error-monitoring service, so we can find and fix it. This does not include your restaurant ratings or personal notes.

We don't sell your information to anyone, for any reason.

Who can see what

  • Your trust ratings of other people are private. Only you ever see who you've rated and how highly.
  • Your restaurant ratings and notes are visible to the people you're connected with in the app, and may factor into recommendations shown to other users (as an anonymized "community" or "web" pick), without revealing your identity to a stranger who's never connected with you.
  • Your name is visible to people you're directly connected with, and to anyone you send an invite to.

Your choices

  • You can decline location access at any time and still use the app fully.
  • You can delete individual entries you've posted.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated data by reaching out through the feedback link in the app — we'll remove what we can, though some anonymized or aggregated information (like an entry that's already factored into someone else's recommendation) may not be fully reversible after the fact.

Children's privacy

Nate-Worthy is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, reach out through the feedback link and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you've accepted the update.

Questions

The best way to reach us is through the feedback link inside the app itself.
